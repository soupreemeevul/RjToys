Meet the Maker

Behind RJ Toys is a passion for creating one-of-a-kind custom action figures. Through kitbashing, customization, and careful attention to detail, familiar characters are reimagined as unique, handcrafted pieces.

From completed figures to projects still taking shape on the workbench, RJ Toys offers a look into the creativity and craftsmanship behind each build.
